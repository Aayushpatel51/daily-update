# Brand foundation

Status: proposed direction, September 11, 2026. Daily Update is a working name; domain, trademark availability, logo, and final naming have not been researched or selected.

## Positioning

For people following fast-moving technology fields, Daily Update provides timely, verified briefs and clear explanations of developments they choose to follow.

Core promise: **Know what changed. Understand why it matters.**

Channel roles:

- Telegram: the useful first brief.
- Website: context, evidence, and a durable reference.
- Email: the day's developments, gathered by topic.

## Personality

Informed, calm, direct, curious, and transparent. Write as an attentive editor, not an excited promoter. Help readers feel capable rather than anxious about falling behind.

Do not claim to cover everything, deliver without delay, or make creators first. Avoid artificial urgency, unsupported superlatives, fabricated readership numbers, and fear-based streaks.

## Editorial voice

- Lead with the concrete development and affected audience.
- Explain unfamiliar terms briefly; do not assume every reader is an engineer.
- Attribute performance and business claims to their source.
- State availability, region, and eligibility where evidence supports them.
- Separate facts, interpretation, and unresolved questions.
- Use absolute dates when relative language could become confusing in archived content.
- Link original sources rather than making readers search for evidence.
- Correct errors plainly and visibly.

### Example copy

Good headline: “Company X opens access to its new coding model.”

Good relevance: “Developers can now test it through the API; the announcement does not include independent performance results.”

Avoid: “This insane AI release changes everything. You cannot afford to miss it.”

These examples are fictional editorial examples, not current news.

## Content patterns

| Surface | Pattern |
| --- | --- |
| Telegram | Headline → what happened → why it matters → evidence label/source → article link when available |
| Article | Brief → what changed → who is affected → context → unknowns → sources and updates |
| Daily email | Coverage interval → topic sections → headline and short brief per story → article links → preferences |
| Empty feed | “No published updates in this topic yet.” Do not manufacture sample news without a visible demo label |
| Pending article | “The full explanation is being prepared. Read the original announcement.” |
| Correction | “Correction: [specific fact]. Updated [date/time].” |

## Visual direction

An editorial technology publication: warm light surfaces, dark readable type, a restrained blue accent, generous reading space, and compact chronological lists. Typography and useful information should establish identity before decoration.

Use a text wordmark during the pilot. No generated logo or visual asset is required to start. Topic colors are navigational cues, not evidence of importance. Evidence status must always include text.

Avoid generic AI imagery, glowing brains, animated trading tickers, endless gradients, fabricated charts, and decorative urgency indicators. Use product imagery only when helpful and permission allows; a clear text article is acceptable.

## Naming and brand decisions before public launch

- Select a public name and available domain.
- Confirm audience language and regional tone.
- Decide whether the identity should emphasize broad technology or future multi-field expansion.
- Prepare a source/corrections statement, contact path, and privacy/subscription language consistent with actual operation.
